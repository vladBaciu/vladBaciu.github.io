Welcome to my first blog post! 🎉

This blog is built with [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) and Jekyll, which makes it easy to write posts in Markdown and have them automatically converted to HTML.

## Why I'm Starting a Blog

I believe in sharing knowledge and documenting my journey through embedded systems, machine learning, and security research. This blog will serve as a space to:

- Share detailed technical writeups
- Document interesting projects
- Discuss research findings
- Provide tutorials and tips

## What to Expect

Over time, you'll find posts covering:

- **Technical deep-dives** into embedded systems and firmware development
- **Machine Learning applications** on edge devices
- **Project updates** and learnings from my repositories
- **Tips and tricks** from my experience with microcontrollers and signal processing
- **Research insights** on photoplethysmography and biomedical signal processing

## The Blog Structure

The blog is organized into several sections:

- **Home** - Welcome page with latest posts
- **Blog** - Archive of all blog posts
- **Projects** - Showcase of my GitHub repositories
- **About** - More information about me and my research focus
